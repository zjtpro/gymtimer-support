「健身计时」（以下简称"本 App"）非常重视您的隐私。本隐私政策说明本 App 如何处理（或不处理）您的信息。

### 1. 我们不收集任何个人信息
本 App **完全离线运行**，不会收集、上传、存储或共享任何个人身份信息或行为数据。具体而言：
- 不需要注册账号或登录
- 不收集您的姓名、邮箱、电话、位置、设备 ID 等任何个人信息
- 不读取或写入"健康"App 中的任何健康数据
- 不集成任何第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK
- 不进行任何网络通信，不向任何服务器发送数据

### 2. 本地数据
本 App 仅在您的设备本地保存以下配置信息，仅用于让您下次打开 App 时保留上次的设置：
- 上次设定的训练组数
- 上次设定的组间休息时长

这些数据通过 iOS 的 UserDefaults 机制存储在您的设备本地，**不会离开您的设备**。卸载 App 即自动删除。

### 3. 健康数据权限
本 App 会请求 HealthKit 权限，但**不会读取或写入任何健康数据**。该权限仅用于在 Apple Watch 上启动一个空的 HKWorkoutSession，以保证休息结束时的震动提醒在息屏或腕部静止状态下仍能可靠触发。Session 结束时不会向"健康"App 添加任何记录。

### 4. 通知权限
本 App 会请求通知权限，仅用于在 App 退到后台时，于休息倒计时结束的时刻发送本地通知提醒您。通知由您的设备本地生成，不经过任何服务器。

### 5. 儿童隐私
本 App 不针对 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童的任何信息。

## English

GymTimer ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles (or does not handle) your information.

### 1. We Do Not Collect Any Personal Data
The App runs **fully offline** and does NOT collect, upload, store, or share any personally identifiable information or behavioral data. Specifically:
- No account registration or sign-in is required.
- We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, device identifiers, or any personal data.
- We do not read from or write to any health data in the Apple Health app.
- We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- The App does not perform any network requests; no data is ever sent to any server.

### 2. Local Data
The App saves only the following configuration locally on your device, solely to preserve your settings for next launch:
- Last configured number of training sets
- Last configured rest duration in seconds

These values are stored via iOS UserDefaults on your device. **They never leave your device** and are automatically removed when you uninstall the App.

### 3. HealthKit Permission
The App requests HealthKit permission but **does NOT read or write any health data**. The permission is solely used to start an empty HKWorkoutSession on Apple Watch, ensuring the end-of-rest haptic alert fires reliably when the screen is off or the wrist is down. No workout records are ever written to the Health app.

### 4. Notification Permission
The App requests notification permission solely to send a local notification when your rest countdown ends while the App is in the background. Notifications are generated locally on your device and never go through any server.

### 5. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above. Please check this page periodically.
